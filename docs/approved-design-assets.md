# Approved design assets

These are project-local assets generated with the built-in image-generation tool to implement the
user-approved ten-phone mockup. The approval supersedes the earlier no-generated-imagery direction.
No graphic logo was introduced. All four images remain unedited; CSS frames isolate each contact-sheet item.

- `public/assets/collection-v3/hero.png`: dark navy hero, three full concept phones on slate, left negative space.
- `public/assets/collection-v3/phones.png`: ten distinct phone concepts in a 5-column, 2-row contact sheet.
- `public/assets/collection-v3/accessories.png`: cases, wall charger with cable, wireless stand and earbuds in a 2x2 sheet.
- `public/assets/collection-v3/detail.png`: close-up brushed titanium camera surround on a navy background.

## Final prompt set

Hero: Premium photorealistic 3:2 website background; no text or logos; midnight navy, slate at the bottom,
three concept phones on the right in brushed titanium, ivory and midnight; ocean screen artwork;
left 40% empty for the headline, subtle natural studio rim lighting.

Phones: Photorealistic 5x2 contact sheet, equal ivory cells, no text, borders or logos; full devices with
margins. Top row: silver everyday dual camera, slim ivory Air, graphite triple camera Pro,
champagne Pro Max, powder-blue Mini. Bottom: sage Plus, charcoal Studio with camera bar,
ice-blue Edge, open book-style Fold, partially open champagne clamshell Flip. Natural stone/ocean screens.

Accessories: Photorealistic 2x2 equal-cell contact sheet without text or logos. Sand leather-look phone case
on linen; cream wall charger and coiled braided USB-C cable; brushed champagne wireless stand;
cream earbuds and open case. Warm studio daylight, natural stone and linen textures.

Detail: Photorealistic 3:2 macro, midnight navy, brushed champagne titanium phone camera surround filling
the right two thirds, three dark round lenses, slim side button, cool controlled highlights,
left third dark negative space; no logos, text, neon or purple.

The originals remain in the Codex generated-images directory. The published site consumes only the
workspace copies above. These fictional product renders are not evidence of manufactured devices.
